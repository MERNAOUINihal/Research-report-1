# Research Report 1  
## Informal Support Networks and Material Deprivation in Morocco  


## Overview
This project analyzes data from Afrobarometer Round 10 (Morocco, 2024) to examine how material deprivation and neighborhood infrastructure influence households’ reliance on informal support networks and perceptions of insecurity.

## Research Question
How do material deprivation and neighborhood infrastructure conditions shape:
- reliance on informal support networks?
- perceptions of neighborhood insecurity?

## Methodology
- Dataset: Afrobarometer Round 10 (Morocco)  
- Approach: Quantitative analysis  
- Models: Descriptive statistics and Linear Probability Models (LPM)

## Key Variables
- **Material deprivation** (access to basic needs)  
- **Infrastructure conditions** (services and environment)  
- **Informal support networks** (kin vs. non-kin support)  

## Discussion and Conclusion

- The analysis identifies a strong association between material deprivation and reliance on informal support networks in Morocco. However, these findings should be interpreted as correlational rather than causal, as the cross-sectional design does not allow for determining directionality or underlying mechanisms.
- The measurement of deprivation as a binary indicator simplifies a complex and multidimensional phenomenon.
- Evidence regarding changes in network composition is limited.
- Model diagnostics indicate minor specification limitations.
- The study measures the receipt of support rather than the structure, quality, or reliability of social ties.
